# Lesson 03 — Segmentation and measurement

Segment and measure eight labeled pills in a real MEDISEG-Deploy photograph.

## Run-through

Complete Lesson 02 and the two items in [the pre-work](readings.md).

Open [the run-through](03-segmentation-and-measurement.ipynb) with the `ac-cv`
kernel. Record three predictions before the gated cells run. The notebook
compares grayscale and color-aware thresholds, cleans the mask, selects a
measurement from each isolated pill's shape, and saves results under
`results/lesson-03/`.

The workbook is in `lessons/2026-08-06/03-workbook.ipynb`.
